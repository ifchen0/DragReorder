Reorder the bottom bar, the architect categories and the buildings inside each category by dragging them with the right mouse button.

[h2]How to use[/h2]Hold the right mouse button on a button, drag it onto another button and release. The dragged button takes that place.
[list]
[*][b]Bottom bar:[/b] the main buttons (Work, Schedule, Architect, ...).
[*][b]Architect categories:[/b] the category buttons on the left of the architect menu.
[*][b]Buildings:[/b] the buildings inside the open category, including dropdown groups such as floors.
[/list]
While dragging, the button you picked up is dimmed and the drop target is outlined. Releasing anywhere else cancels the drag. A plain right click (without moving the mouse) works exactly as before.

[h2]How it works[/h2]The order is kept in the mod settings and applies to every save. Vanilla's own order is not changed in the game files: the bottom bar and category lists are rearranged in place, and the building grid is re-sorted only while an architect category is drawn, so gizmos of selected pawns and buildings are not affected.

Buttons that a mod adds later are placed next to where they would normally appear, so you do not have to start over after changing your mod list.

[h2]Notes[/h2][list]
[*]Each of the three orders can be reset to default in the mod settings.
[*]Safe to add to or remove from an existing save.
[*]Do not use together with another mod that reorders the same buttons (for example Tab-sorting); both would fight over the order.
[*]If a future RimWorld update changes the building grid code, the mod logs a warning and buildings keep their default order.
[/list]
